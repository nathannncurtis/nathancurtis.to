   BURNT ORANGE

   the leaves were always changing,
   burnt orange, a hue that danced, then drifted,
   one — a single leaf, swayed, paused,
   before resting gently on the ground,
   like a whisper of what's yet to come.

   the seasons shifted, silent, slow,
   autumn blending into winter's grasp.
   yet, there's warmth in the cold,
   a hidden ember beneath the frost,
   a quiet spring waiting, just out of reach.

   a crow, calm, lands on a powerline,
   perched in quiet contemplation,
   its black feathers sharp against the soft orange sky,
   a fleeting moment, like our first hello,
   but lasting — etched in memory.

   carefully, calmly, the crow contemplates,
   clawed feet clutching the cold cable,
   as we navigate this path,
   subtle moments of almost-rhyme,
   but don't, and that's just right.

   the wind, it whispers —
   carrying us forward, gently,
   through seasons, through change.
   the leaf falls again, but not forgotten,
   transforming, as we have,
   in every smile, every shared glance —
   almost like that crow, watching,
   from its wire, as we navigate this path.

   and now, as the crow takes flight,
   the burnt orange fades into something new,
   something we're still discovering, together,
   in this ever-changing season of friendship.
