title: "Roasted Tomatillo Habanero Salsa"
note: "roasted, blistered, and honestly too hot"
serves: "~15"
---

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs tomatillos, husked and rinsed
- 6 habanero peppers, stems removed
- 4 jalapeños, stems removed
- 2 poblano peppers, stems removed and halved
- 1 large white onion, cut into 8 wedges
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1 bunch cilantro (stems removed if thick)
- Juice of 3 limes
- 2 tsp kosher salt (plus more to taste)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (optional)

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. Place the tomatillos (whole), habaneros (whole), jalapeños (whole), poblanos (halved), onion wedges, and garlic cloves on a large baking sheet. Drizzle with oil if using and toss lightly.
3. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning everything once halfway through, until the tomatillos are soft and the vegetables have a good char.
4. Let cool for 5–10 minutes.
5. Add the roasted vegetables, cilantro, lime juice, and 2 teaspoons salt to a blender.
6. Blend until smooth or slightly chunky, depending on your preference. If it's too thick, add 1–3 tablespoons of water.
7. Taste and adjust with more salt or lime juice if needed.
8. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving. The flavor gets noticeably better after a few hours.

## Notes

- This salsa is very spicy. Six habaneros plus four jalapeños will have a strong, lingering heat.
- If it's hotter than you'd like after blending, add another ½–1 pound of roasted tomatillos and a little more salt to mellow it out without changing the flavor much.
- Makes about 5–6 cups of salsa, enough for roughly 15 people as an appetizer with chips.
