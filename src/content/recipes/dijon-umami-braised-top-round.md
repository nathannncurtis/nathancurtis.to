---
title: "Dijon–Umami Braised Top Round"
note: "low and slow until it gives up and pulls apart"
serves: "4–6"
---

A pull-apart braise — Dijon and a quiet trio of umami (Worcestershire, soy, maple) carry a humble top round a long way.

## Ingredients

- 1 beef top round roast (about 2½–3 lb)
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Dijon mustard (about 2 tbsp, divided)
- 1–2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2–3 cloves garlic, minced
- ¾ cup beef broth or water
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 300°F (regular bake, no convection).
2. Pat the roast dry. Season generously on all sides with salt and pepper, then spread a thin layer of Dijon mustard over the surface.
3. Heat oil in a heavy oven-safe pot or cast iron pan over medium-high heat. Sear the roast on all sides until well browned. Transfer to a plate.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook until softened and lightly browned, about 5–7 minutes.
5. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
6. Pour in the broth, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, maple syrup, and remaining Dijon. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
7. Return the roast to the pan. The liquid should come about halfway up the sides of the meat.
8. Cover tightly with a lid or foil and transfer to the oven.
9. Braise for 3 to 3½ hours, checking once around the 2½-hour mark to make sure there is still liquid in the pan. Add a small splash of water if needed.
10. The meat is done when it pulls apart easily with a fork. If it still resists, return it to the oven for another 20–30 minutes.
11. Remove the roast and tent loosely with foil.
12. Simmer the braising liquid on the stovetop until slightly thickened. Taste and adjust seasoning.
13. Shred the beef and return it to the sauce. Toss to coat.
14. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
